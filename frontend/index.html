How often each brand promotes

How often, how deep and how long each brand runs promotions.

How this is measured

How often each brand goes on promo, from every scrape in the price history. A listing counts as on promo on a day when the retailer shows a was-price above the price. A promo is a run of such days; one that's still running is counted too. Display / clearance units are left out. Plaisio doesn't show was-prices, so there a promo is the price sitting 5% or more under its highest price of the previous 30 days (a permanent price cut also reads as a promo for up to 30 days). Days the PC didn't scrape are carried over from the previous scrape, so a weekend-only deal can be missed.

Alerts

A same-specification competitor priced at or below one of your models.

Alert settings

A rival is flagged when it has the same specs and colour as your product (the rules the Price index and the same-specs export use; cooking keeps its size tiers) and is priced at or below yours. Rivals offering a better energy class or more capacity for the money are ranked as high-priority disruptions.

Match review

Confirm or split the listings matched as the same model across retailers.

How this is measured

Cross-retailer matches computed on the server (EAN → MPN → model code → fuzzy). Confirm merges the listings as one model everywhere; “Not the same” splits them — your verdicts are permanent and always win over recomputation. AI-review pre-sorts the queue: it recommends confident merges (one-click Accept) and flags the doubtful ones for you.
