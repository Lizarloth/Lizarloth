What Pricedge does

Pricedge continuously tracks product pricing, availability and specifications across leading Greek retailers, then turns that into a clear competitive picture — price parity across stores, market segments by size and class, brand positioning, and timely alerts when the market moves. It is built for brand and category teams who need to understand where their products stand and act quickly.
